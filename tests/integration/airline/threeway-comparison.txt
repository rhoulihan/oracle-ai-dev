Sparse type-filtered indexes + auto-partition-by-type cut the single-table write
from 3084 to 1805 redo (~41%) and its read to 3 logical reads (partition prune +
sparse index range scan). Reads are now IDENTICAL across all three shapes; writes
are close — relational 1440 < document 1631 < single-table 1805 (within ~25%).
